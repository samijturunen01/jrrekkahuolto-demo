# Conversion strategy – JR Rekkahuolto Oy

Verified facts are marked ✔; everything else is a strategic assumption to validate with the customer and real data.

## Business model

B2B-focused heavy-vehicle workshop ✔ ("raskaan kaluston huoltoon ja korjauksiin erikoistunut"). Revenue comes from repair and maintenance work on trucks and trailers, transport refrigeration/heating units, tail lifts and hydraulics, inspections and inspection runs ✔. Work is agreed per job – no public prices ✔. Customers typically call (assumption: the phone is the main channel today, as the current site offers no other CTA).

## Primary customer segments (assumptions)

1. **Transport and logistics companies with fleets** – need predictable maintenance, minimal downtime, one supplier for vehicle + trailer + reefer + tail lift.
2. **Owner-drivers (single-truck entrepreneurs)** – urgent repairs, inspection deadlines, flexible hours.
3. **Distribution and cold-chain operators** – reefer units (Thermo King, Carrier, Lumikko…) and tail lifts are business-critical.
4. **Fleet managers / transport planners** – compare providers, need contact persons and capacity facts.

## Main pain points

- Downtime: a vehicle in the workshop is not earning.
- Finding a slot that fits around driving shifts.
- Specialist equipment (reefer, tail lift, tachograph) needing separate suppliers.
- Inspection risk: failed inspection → re-inspection and more downtime.
- Not knowing whom to call.

## Verified strengths used in the messaging

- Open ma–pe 7–20, la 7–15, other times by agreement ✔
- Two 30 m drive-through lanes, 25 m pit with lift, 20 m lane with movable column lifts ✔
- Vehicle + trailer + reefer/heater + AC + tail lift + hydraulics + inspections under one roof ✔ (since 5.6.2024)
- Heavy-vehicle specialist since 2005 ✔; rebuilt hall and equipment 2014 ✔
- Named foremen with direct numbers ✔

## Conversion goals

- **Primary:** a qualified service request – a call to the workshop or a submitted "huoltopyyntö" (CTA "Pyydä huoltoaikaa").
- **Secondary:** direct call to a foreman, email, directions/map, service page views, hall plan interaction, FAQ reads.

## Customer journey

1. **Attention** – hero: clear offer + Joensuu + hours; façade intro brands the moment.
2. **Value** – facts strip (7–20, 2 × 30 m, 25 m pit, since 2005).
3. **Services** – numbered service index → dedicated pages.
4. **Trust** – "Auki arkisin kello 20:een" section, data plate, hall plan, real photos, history timeline.
5. **Objections** – FAQ (length, hours, inspections, price, whom to call), "Kerro yhteydenotossa" checklist.
6. **Action** – CTA band on every page, sticky mobile call bar, preselected request form, direct numbers.

Journeys tested (internal heuristic, not user research): urgent breakdown on mobile → tap-to-call in header within one screen; reefer problem → Lämmönsäätölaitteet page → "Soita: Jari 050 375 1080"; inspection due → Katsastus page → 3-step flow → request with topic preselected; fleet manager comparing → Korjaamo page (facility, history, people).

## Main conversion barriers and responses

| Barrier | Response |
|---|---|
| No CTA on the current site | Primary CTA in header, hero, sections, every page's closing band; sticky mobile bar |
| Phone numbers not clickable | All numbers `tel:` links, foremen included |
| Services described in four words | Service pages with included work, situations, FAQ |
| Specialist services hidden in a news box | Equal-weight service pages + Jari's direct line |
| Unclear capacity | Hall plan + facts (30 m, 25 m pit) |
| Price uncertainty | Honest explanation of what affects price + "ask for an estimate" |
| Slow mobile journey | Mobile-first layout, ≤ 1 tap to call |

## Conversion hypotheses (to be measured, not promised)

1. Showing "auki arkisin 7–20" above the fold increases calls outside 8–16 office hours.
2. Tap-to-call in the mobile header + sticky bar increases phone-click rate on mobile.
3. Direct foreman numbers on reefer/tail-lift pages increase specialist enquiries.
4. Topic-preselected request forms produce more complete, better-qualified requests.
5. The hall plan and drive-through facts reduce "will my vehicle fit?" calls and increase fleet enquiries.
