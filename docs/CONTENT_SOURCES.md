## Verified history (yritys.php)

- "Joensuun Rekkahuolto Oy on vuonna 2005 perustettu raskaan kaluston huoltoon ja korjauksiin erikoistunut yritys."
- 2013: a fire destroyed the premises practically completely; operations paused for almost a year.
- May 2014: operations resumed in new premises with renewed equipment.
- Name changed to JR Rekkahuolto Oy after a change in ownership (YTJ: 20.10.2015).
- 5.6.2024: bought the business of Jaleka Huoltopalvelut → transport refrigeration/heating units (Thermo King, Lumikko, Carrier, Webasto "yms."), air conditioning, tail lifts and other hydraulics; plus tachograph and speed-limiter inspections, pre-inspection checks and taking vehicles to inspection.

## Verified facility (yritys.php)

- Two 30 m long drive-through service lanes; one has a 25 m service pit and a lift ("nosturi").
- A 20 m service lane with column lifts that can also be moved elsewhere in the workshop.

The real floor plan and lane arrangement are **not** published. The site's hall plan is labelled "Havainnekuva" and states that the arrangement is illustrative; only the lengths are to scale.

## Verified services (palvelut.php + etusivu "Ajankohtaista")

1. Autojen ja perävaunujen korjaukset: moottori, voimansiirto, akselit, valot, alusta jne.
2. Kuormatilojen korjaukset
3. Piirtureiden ja nopeudenrajoittimien tarkastukset, katsastustarkastukset ja katsastuksessa käyttö
4. Lämmönsäätölaitteiden huollot ja korjaukset – Thermo King, Lumikko, Carrier, Webasto, Eber yms.
5. Ohjaamoilmastointien huollot ja korjaukset
6. Perälautojen huollot ja korjaukset; hydrauliikan huollot ja korjaukset

The redesign groups these into five service pages (kuormatilat + perälaudat + hydrauliikka share one page).

## Requires customer confirmation

- **Founding year.** The website says 2005; YTJ shows the business ID registered 19.1.2004 (as Joensuun Rekkahuolto Oy, name registered 29.1.2004). The site uses 2005 (the company's own statement). Confirm which year the customer wants to use.
- **Tachograph inspections.** JR Rekkahuolto Oy (1874798-9) is **not** listed in Traficom's "Ajopiirturikorjaamot 2025" list (checked 23.11.2026). The service is described exactly as on the current site, without claiming Traficom authorisation. Confirm how tachograph inspections are carried out (own authorisation, partner, or wording to change).
- **Brands.** "Thermoking" on the current site is written in its official form "Thermo King". "Eber" presumably refers to Eberspächer heaters – kept as "Eber" as on the current site.
- **Risto Mäkisalo's area of responsibility** is not stated – shown only as "Työnjohtaja".
- **Holiday opening hours** are not published; the site's "open now" indicator follows the regular hours and advises confirming holiday hours by phone.
- **010 number pricing.** 010 numbers are service numbers with operator-specific pricing. Finnish practice is to show the call price next to the number – the price is not published on the current site; add it in production.
- **Washing.** The current site's carousel contains three photos of a truck wash (Tammermatic). Washing is not listed as a service anywhere, so these photos are excluded and washing is not mentioned. Confirm whether washing is a service of JR Rekkahuolto.
- **Pricing.** No prices are published. The site explains that the price depends on the vehicle, scope and parts, and directs to ask for an estimate. No prices were invented.
- **Response times / booking process.** Not published – the site makes no response-time promises.
- **Vehicle brands serviced.** Not stated on the current site – the site does not claim to be brand-independent or authorised by any manufacturer.
